Features and default balance

Project Requiem 0.3.0-pre.5 adds a hardcore survival preset to Resident Evil Requiem PC through REFramework. English is the default; Vietnamese is selectable. This document describes the shipped values and fixed rules. The release remains experimental. See verification for the difference between automated checks and observed behavior in the game.

Combat and finite bonuses

Mechanic Default factor
Enemy effective toughness 1.80
Incoming player damage 1.45
Player weapon damage 0.70
Enemy movement speed 1.10
Enemy stagger/wince 0.65
Minimum firing interval 1.35
Reload animation duration 1.50

Toughness divides damage into registered enemy HitPoint objects. Combined with weapon damage, enemies receive about 38.9% of native damage before integer rounding. It does not rewrite maximum HP or boss phase thresholds. Melee and environmental damage using the same HP function are also affected. The mod does not add AI or enemy spawns.

Speed and stagger use experimental rank getters that may be inlined or bypassed. A call counter does not prove a visible effect on every enemy. Firing intervals use a 0.25-second base if native weapon data is unreadable. Reload scaling preserves native animation events and avoids compounding the factor each frame.

While enabled, the mod forces off built-in infinite gun/rocket ammunition and the shared infinite axe/knife durability bonus. It also clears equipped loader panels' applicable infinite/zero-cost flags. Getter overrides and boolean-field enforcement are combined. External trainers or conflicting mods can bypass these paths.

Healing and world scarcity

Resource rule Default
Recognized world healing pickups retained About 40%
Item healing retained at Stress 0 45%
Item healing retained at Stress 100 33.75%
Recognized world ink ribbons retained About 45%

Keep/hide decisions are stable for the same seed, run ID, item and identified location. Revisiting does not reroll them. These percentages describe many locations, not a guaranteed fraction in each room.

Scarcity applies to recognized world pickups, not inventory transfers, crafting, shops or inventory restoration. Unknown IDs, items without stable location identity, and key/story items remain available. Nested recovery calls are scaled once; healing modifiers do not lower maximum HP.

Stress Meter and nearby threats

Stress ranges from 0 to 100 and worsens survival and weapon handling.

Source Default gain
Living, present enemy within 12 m 1.8/second
Player below 30% HP 0.9/second
Nearby threat with recognized player light off Another 0.35/second
Taking damage 8 plus HP-loss contribution
Registered physical shot 0.7
No continuing source Recover 0.65/second

Damage adds up to another 15 Stress according to the fraction of maximum HP lost. Continuing source types add together; multiple nearby enemies do not multiply the proximity gain. The light condition tests whether the recognized light is on; it does not measure environmental darkness.

Threat scans combine and deduplicate enemy/context lists, preferring current rendered transforms. A living, physically present suspended Licker can count before waking or attacking. Corpses, inactive objects, despawned contexts without an object and unreadable positions are excluded. Scans start with a valid player and repeat about every 0.25 seconds. The radius is fixed at 12 m.

Reload-duration factor 1.50 1.875
Reticle recovery multiplier 1.00 0.45

Values interpolate continuously. HUD colors change at Stress 45 and 75. The reticle-recovery method may be unused/inlined; panic has additional enforcement paths.

Panic at Stress 100

Panic starts at 100 and lasts until Stress reaches 90 or less. It applies to the recognized active Grace or Leon character. Kind IDs provide a fallback when native character flags are unavailable.

  • Reticle fitting is cleared after native updates, with its cached fit point driven to the weapon's minimum.
  • A compatible reflected projectile direction receives a 2.5-degree deviation cone while preserving its length. Otherwise the final shell rotation receives roughly 2.5 degrees of pitch/yaw deviation. Time and shot count vary direction; a creation scope avoids applying both paths to one shot. Unlike the old cached target, these paths do not depend on a target that shell creation can overwrite.
  • Render-camera sway is bounded at 0.6 degrees pitch, 0.7 degrees yaw and 1.5 degrees roll. The original rotation is restored before gameplay simulation, on recovery and on disable/reset. It does not accumulate drift or replace a later engine rotation.
  • Slow haze and breathing tunnel vision remain visible when the HUD is hidden. They are not a blur filter or an exact copy of Leon's postprocess.
  • Supplementary fear/sick hooks target only the current player. Sick-state enforcement requires that character's valid configuration; Leon's animations and configuration are not transplanted onto Grace.

Panic persists with Stress, suspends during pause/inactive gameplay and releases on recovery or mod disable. Aim updates do not consume jam/ink RNG. Every weapon's actual trajectory and character-specific native visuals still need native verification.

Heat and weapon jams

Each registered shot adds 0.18 heat, capped at 1. Heat cools by 0.14/second of live gameplay. No jam roll occurs below 0.40 heat. At or above that threshold:

jam chance = 0.16 * heat * (0.5 + 0.5 * Stress / 100)

One roll follows each registered shot. Holding input or per-frame updates cannot reroll it. A jam gates fire requests, native fire eligibility, projectile creation and further ammunition consumption. Deferred callbacks do not count phantom shots while jammed.

Use the game's Reload action, or R as a keyboard fallback, to clear the jam. Clearing takes 3-4.5 live seconds, even with a full magazine, and leaves heat at 0.20. Switching weapons does not clear a jam.

Heat/jams belong to individual player/weapon objects for the Lua session. Resetting scripts, restarting or loading that discards the player context can clear them. Total shots/jams persist; individual hot/jammed objects do not.

Stamina and exhaustion

The recognized active Grace or Leon player has a 0-100 Stamina Meter.

Rule Default
Capacity and starting stamina 100
Sprint drain 16/second
Uninterrupted sprint from full 6.25 seconds
Recovery delay after sprint 2 live seconds
Resting recovery 12/second
Moving recovery 6/second
Exhausted walking playback 60%
Exhausted run-transition playback 40%
Exhaustion release 25 stamina

Zero stamina latches exhaustion and blocks sprint. Holding sprint while exhausted does not prevent recovery; normal walking costs no stamina. After native input, the mod cancels sprint and requests walking. It also scales the active locomotion layer without compounding, then restores its original speed on release.

Terrain actions, damage reactions, riding and reloads are excluded. Movement speed is restored before the separate reload modifier applies. Global time and camera input are unchanged. Stamina, exhaustion and recovery delay persist using the same live-time guards as Stress and lights.

FPS/TPS, stairs, crouching, both characters and engine paths bypassing the identified movement/input methods still require native validation. Playback factors are not a measurement of actual native movement velocity.

One Life (Strict)

Enable One Life is optional and off by default. It can be disabled while alive. Death permanently locks the attempt. Enabling it in an existing campaign trusts no old resume slots: make a successful new manual save before quitting to resume that living attempt. Quota, cooldown and ink rules still apply.

  • Native current-player death callbacks and a dead/zero-HP fallback persist death immediately. Enemy deaths do not count.
  • After death, gameplay Continue/Load requests are denied, including category and final segment/slot overloads. While alive, only manual slots confirmed by successful saves in this attempt can resume. Unknown slots, autosaves and earlier attempts' slots are denied. Resolved system/profile loads are allowed.
  • The run-ended notice survives hidden HUD, general mod disable, script reset and restart. General disable does not bypass an armed One Life attempt.
  • If an in-memory checkpoint respawns the player, the mod requests native game over and guards recognized movement/fire paths. It does not delete checkpoints or replace the native game-over screen.
  • Choose native New Game from the title screen to start another attempt. Actual setup resets resources, Stress, stamina, quota/cooldown, ink/RNG history and trusted slots, and increments the attempt. Opening/cancelling New Game does not reset it. The selected One Life mode carries forward.

Native saves are not deleted or corrupted. Previous-attempt slots become usable only if successfully overwritten in the new attempt. Local config, ledger edits, backups or removing the mod can bypass rules; there is no leaderboard or tamper protection. Death, New Game, checkpoint paths and native value-type slot identity have automated coverage, not play-testing.

One Life (Easy)

Choose Enable One Life (Easy) during live gameplay with a valid calm Continue point. Before activation, the update thread deploys every current inventory entry's native ItemAmountData, encodes/decodes it and compares the round trip. Unsupported data refuses activation. The check repeats at death because inventory may have changed.

Rule Behavior
Continue action F6 or menu's Continue (Easy)
Continue HP 50% of current maximum; at least 1
Recover action F7 or menu button
Recovery range Within 2 m; same player and area
Outstanding cache limit 128
Full inventory Unclaimed entries stay in cache

This is soft death, not native game-over Continue. Supported paths are lethal addDamageHitPoint after incoming scaling and current-player forceDead. Before irreversible death, the mod blocks movement, firing and further HP damage. Continue warps to the last calm locomotion point for the same character/native resume-area ID and resets HP through captured APIs.

The in-memory world continues without loading a save or rewinding progress. It can keep advancing while the defeat prompt is open. Stress, stamina, lights, elapsed time and save quota/cooldown keep their current values. Continue makes no manual-save, load or autosave request.

All carried panels and archived inventory items, including keys, move to a persistent cache at the recorded death position. A world-space marker, distance label and recovery action represent the cache; no loose item models are spawned. Weapon data includes loaded rounds and attachments; attached panels are not duplicated as loose items. Item boxes, file inventories and separate wallet currency are outside the carried inventory returned by GuiUtil.getInventory. Later deaths retain unclaimed caches; completed caches are pruned.

Transfers are journaled before removal. Native inventory snapshots permit rollback if removal or persistence fails. Pickup checks capacity, journals the claim and verifies the full stock increase before claiming the entry. Unsupported, cyclic, oversized or ambiguous interrupted data is rejected. If rollback fails, the transaction/error remains for reconciliation. This does not provide crash-proof atomicity between native memory and JSON files.

Continue is session-based: the ledger cannot preserve the entire live world after quitting. Death revokes earlier trusted resume slots. After Continue, make a successful manual save under normal rules before quitting to resume later. Pending defeats/cache journals survive Lua resets but do not replace game saves. New Game resets the attempt and cache list. Pending defeat or outstanding loot prevents menu mode changes/disabling One Life.

No complete native inventory/serialization/warp/recovery cycle has been observed. Scripted executions, cutscenes, falls or deaths bypassing the guards may cause irreversible death and end the attempt. Calm points are not engine checkpoints or validated navmesh locations. One-way routes, streaming changes, character switches and story progress may prevent cache recovery. Easy remains experimental.

Flashlight and lighter limits

Source Capacity Unlock after depletion
Flashlight / BodyLight 180 seconds 27 energy-seconds
Lighter 90 seconds 13.5 energy-seconds

Separate pools recover 0.15 energy-seconds per live second while off, up to capacity. Unlock requires 15% of capacity: from empty, about 180 live seconds for the flashlight or 90 for the lighter.

At zero, the mod blocks use and overrides the player's emitter enable and visibility flags after native updates. It avoids external off requests and intensity changes that could hold a recovered light off. Overrides preserve fresh controller-written state and restore it on release/disable. Normal player toggling resumes afterward.

While depleted, the HUD shows energy against the unlock threshold and announces readiness. A small positive value alone does not unlock the light. The recharge latch survives reset/restart. Legacy ledgers below the threshold migrate as recharging without resetting energy.

There are no new battery/fuel items or animations, and scene lighting is unchanged. Automated emitter tests do not establish visual extinction for every native light variant.

Live inventory and item box

Recognized Grace/Leon inventory, item-box and inventory crafting, tab, background and description pause requests are disabled. Enemies and time can continue while managing items. Esc pause, loading and cutscenes retain native behavior. Special subdialogs may use other pause paths.

Autosaves, manual saves and spoiled ink

Automatic gameplay saving is forbidden while the mod is enabled. There is no separate autosave-enable setting. Auto/custom gameplay categories 6-9 are denied, with a segment/slot guard. A resolved Auto slot is blocked even inside a manual call. Unclassified requests without valid category scope are denied.

System/profile settings writes remain available without consuming quota. Existing autosaves remain on disk. In-memory checkpoints are not removed; chapter transitions and checkpoint retry behavior are not fully verified.

Manual-save rule Default
Successful saves per run ID At most 12
Cooldown after success 900 live seconds / 15 minutes
Fresh run's first save No initial cooldown
Spoiled ink chance 25% per eligible ribbon transaction

Manual saves must use the typewriter interface and pass all rules. Where native saving needs a ribbon, a real finite ribbon is required; an infinite ribbon bonus cannot replace it. The first save still needs other conditions.

Spoiled ink consumes one ribbon, refuses the write and prevents refund. The HUD reports failure; the game may show its own save-error dialog. The transaction result is remembered so repeated checks cannot reroll it. Quota/cooldown rejection retains the native refund path. Failed saves do not consume successful-save quota or start a success cooldown; only the native successful-write callback increments quota.

Spoilage applies where the game already requires ribbons, usually Grace's sections. The mod does not add a ribbon requirement elsewhere or corrupt or delete existing saves.

Time, HUD and language

Resources, cooldowns, heat and Stress use identified live gameplay time. Timers stop for reported pause/loading, invalid/dead/suspended players, cutscene invalidation, unfocus/minimize where detectable, and stalls above 0.5 seconds. Successfully unpaused inventory continues those timers.

The HUD at (28, 80) shows Stress, stamina/exhaustion, One Life status, light energy, save count, finite/autosave restrictions, jam/panic notices and the nearest recognized threat in range. Hiding it leaves gameplay and panic visuals active; a dead One Life notice stays visible.

Choose English / Tiếng Việt in the menu. Both languages cover HUD, controls and notifications; pending notifications use the current language. Noto Sans applies only to this mod's UI. Engine IDs, JSON keys and diagnostic events retain stable English identifiers.

Pre.5 corrects the combo's one-based Lua keys: English is 1, Vietnamese is 2. Selection saves immediately and reloads without resetting the run. Switching and persistence have automated coverage; native UI verification is pending.

Persistence and diagnostics

Configuration and run ledgers live in reframework/data/project_requiem/. The ledger stores Stress/panic, stamina/exhaustion/recovery delay, lights, elapsed time, save quota/cooldown, RNG/ink decisions, One Life activation/death/attempt/trusted slots and total counters. It flushes about every 15 gameplay seconds and on relevant save/config/reset/report callbacks. One Life death, mode changes, New Game and confirmed One Life saves flush immediately. Sidecar writes are not gameplay autosaves. Crashes or failed disk writes can lose state.

Loading earlier native progress does not refund a run_id ledger. New Game starts a fresh attempt in that ID. Reports export after 120 frames, every 1,800 frames or through the menu. They include:

  • Hook calls/errors, applied events and resource/timer state.
  • Stamina/exhaustion, movement and applied/base locomotion speed.
  • One Life death, attempt, trusted slots and Easy/cache state.
  • Weapon/jam/panic, character eligibility, camera sway and reflected projectile-request fields.
  • Light control/renderer writes, bonus flags and blocked save categories.
  • Threat sources/counts/distances and selected language.

Reflection checks signatures without fixed memory offsets. The installer backs up owned files, migrates old names and preserves unrelated mods. Removal retains data and modified files. Code uses MIT; the bundled font has its own OFL license.
